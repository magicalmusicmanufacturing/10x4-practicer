Rhythm Check

Press play, count along with the click, and play the exercise. With a MIDI keyboard connected, every note is checked for the right key at the right moment.

After each run, notes are colored: green is the right key on time, amber is the right key but early or late, red is missed or wrong. Click any bar to start from there. Shortcuts: Space play/pause, R restart, N next exercise. MIDI keyboards work in Chrome or Edge on a computer, and Chrome on Android.
